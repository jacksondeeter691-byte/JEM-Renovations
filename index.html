Common Questions

Everything you might want to know before reaching out.

How do I get a quote? +
Just give us a call or fill out the contact form below. We'll schedule a time to assess your project and provide a free, no-obligation estimate — usually within 1–2 business days.
What areas do you serve? +
We primarily serve Fishers, IN and surrounding communities including Carmel, Noblesville, Westfield, Zionsville, and Indianapolis. Not sure if we cover your area? Just ask!
Are you licensed and insured? +
Yes — JEM Renovations is fully licensed and insured. We take your safety and trust seriously and are happy to provide documentation upon request.
What size projects do you take on? +
We handle everything from smaller repairs and paint jobs to full bathroom and kitchen remodels, siding replacements, and custom built-in projects. If you're not sure whether your project is a fit, reach out and we'll let you know.
How quickly can you get started? +
It depends on current availability, but we do our best to accommodate your timeline. After your estimate is approved, we'll get you scheduled as quickly as possible and keep you updated throughout.
Do you stand behind your work? +
Absolutely. Your satisfaction is our top priority. If something isn't right, Jamie comes back to fix it — no questions asked. We don't consider a job done until you love the result.
