Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

JEM Renovations LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or contact us for services.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name, phone number, and email address when you submit our contact form or call us directly.
  • Project details — information you voluntarily provide about your renovation or repair project.
  • Usage data — general browsing data such as pages visited and time spent on the site, collected anonymously through standard web analytics.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your estimate requests and inquiries.
  • Schedule consultations and project work.
  • Communicate updates related to your project.
  • Improve our website and services.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Our website uses HTTPS encryption. Form submissions are processed through secure third-party services. We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill your request or as required by law.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party tools such as Google Fonts (for typography) and form submission services. These providers have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data.

Cookies

Our site may use basic cookies to support site functionality. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at (317) 430-0149.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
